# DeskBuddy

**A simple, flexible classroom seating plan maker for teachers.**

Create and rearrange pupil desks, display useful information, and highlight pupils with conditional colour rules. Switch between a practical teacher view and a more colourful pupil view.

### [Launch DeskBuddy](https://trunten.github.io/seating-plan/)

## Features

- **Flexible layouts:** Add desks, drag them into position, auto-arrange them, and rotate the teacher desk.
- **Pupil data:** Import a CSV, edit pupil information, and choose which fields appear on each desk.
- **Conditional colours:** Highlight pupils using rules based on imported fields, with a clickable colour key for filtering.
- **Two views:** Teacher view for detailed information and pupil view with colourful desks.
- **Mobile friendly:** Lock the plan to scroll and pan without accidentally moving desks; tap a desk to see its full details.
- **Zoom:** Show more or less of the room without disturbing your seating layout.
- **Export:** Print an A4 landscape seating plan, export to Excel, or download a reusable DeskBuddy plan file.

## Getting started

1. **Open [DeskBuddy](https://trunten.github.io/seating-plan/)** and give your plan a name.
2. **Add pupils** by importing a CSV, or use **+ Empty desk** and enter their details in the **Data** tab. A `Name` column is supported, as are separate first/last name columns.
3. **Arrange the room** by dragging desks, or choose **Auto arrange desks** under **Plan** settings.
4. **Customise** the information shown under **Desk**, and create highlighting rules under **Colours**.
5. **Save** the plan, switch views, print it, or export it when you're ready.

## Saving your plans

**Save** stores plans in your browser on the current device. They are not automatically synced between devices or browsers.

For a backup or to move a plan to another device, use the **Download** button to export a `.seatplan.json` file. You can import that file back into DeskBuddy later.

DeskBuddy runs in your browser and requires no account or installation.
